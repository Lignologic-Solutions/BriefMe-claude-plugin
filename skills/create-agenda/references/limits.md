# Limits and retention

- Hard limits: 40 blocks and 8 hours per agenda; each block runs 30 seconds to 4 hours.
- Titles: agenda up to 120 characters, block up to 80, description up to 280.
- Agendas expire after 7 days and cannot be recovered afterwards. Agendas saved to a linked BriefMe account are the exception: they do not expire.
- Without a linked account, an agenda whose edit token is lost cannot be recovered; create_agenda makes a new one. A share link does not grant edit access.
